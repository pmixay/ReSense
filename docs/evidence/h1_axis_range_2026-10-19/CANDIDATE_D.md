# Candidate D (registered before implementation)

The C trace is equal to its plain control on 364 paired frame results: 18
standing cases x 14 scans plus two 56-scan approaches. At 109 m left, the
executed robust-core mask contains zero points despite 33/36 observed candidate
returns. At 137 m both sides, the executed boundary interval returns None.
These are geometry/support failures, not the confirmation delay.

D requires a fresh unsmoothed near-rail measurement from the current raw frame,
using the ordinary ridge finder, with at least two paired slabs and sufficient
ridge prominence. Its midpoint residual range relative to the final fitted axis,
expanded by one configured lateral rail-profile bin, supplies an *absolute*
near anchor. Include the original axis (zero) in the hull. Shift every boundary
continuation hypothesis by every anchor endpoint. No current rail support means
no collision-core exemption; a held floor is not fresh anchor evidence.

This shares the floor/rail algorithm with the normal model and is not a certified
truth bound. Raw-point fixture calculations and manufactured common-mode/yaw
errors will test its actual limitations, rather than claiming independence from
every possible model failure.

With that anchor, reduce the additional body erosion from 0.30 to 0.05 m;
the ordinary range-growing gauge margin and all interval hypotheses still apply.
The old 0.30 m was an uncalibrated substitute for an unavailable absolute bound.
At long range, two distinct vertical returns spanning at least 0.15 m may supply
a boundary bin (instead of four raw returns). Still require >=4 near bins and
>=2 local stations for each of the four boundary profiles. Duplicate echoes alone
cannot satisfy the vertical-span requirement.

Component positives now require actual rail geometry in the raw scene. The
unchanged no-rail case45 XYZ is still a fixture hash/shape positive control, but
can no longer prove an absolute track axis; report that scope change explicitly.
Full-detector standing positives keep the existing observed-rail scene, without
an injected truth model. No global axis refit, missing-hit inference, faster
confirmation or retired free-top switch is part of D.

Accept only after all previous full-detector positives and critical matched sets
are retained, outside +/-0.6 m controls reject, and the registered gate passes.
If D fails, preserve its exact source/results and keep it out of active defaults.
