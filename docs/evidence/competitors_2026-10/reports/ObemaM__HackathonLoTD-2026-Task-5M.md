# ObemaM/HackathonLoTD-2026-Task-5M

## Verdict
Classical geometry with a large hand-tuned rule stack (~150 YAML keys) in a C++17 ROS 2 node; self-described experimental baseline with candid docs. Claims (0 alarm frames on 2287 empty frames; synthetic controls 23/24, 20/24, 21/24, 0/24) come from result files not in the repo and from the same 6 bags used for tuning, so they are in-sample.

## Pipeline
1. `detector_node.cpp`: drop NaN / zero / radius outside 0.3-210 m, remap axes, 3-zone voxel (0.05 m to 25 m, 0.10 m to 50 m, 0.20 m beyond).
2. `cloud_processing.cpp:estimate_motion`: ICP on side infrastructure (X 2-45 m, 1.3 < |Y| < 8 m, 0.45 m voxels), gated by fitness, translation, tilt, rotation and coverage; gives ego motion for multi-frame accumulation.
3. `track_path.cpp`: own rail pair per 2 m longitudinal bin from transverse slices; greedy route with continuity penalty, prior from previous frame, and rail-history reacquisition; stops at `ambiguous_branch` when two rail candidates diverge.
4. `tunnel_corridor.cpp`: optional two-wall axis (4 m bins, lateral 1.35-4 m, width 3.4-5.4 m) up to 100 m.
5. Curved 2.1 x 3.0 m corridor on the trusted path; narrow rail mask; longitudinal mask (low lines that persist over >= 8 bins of 2 m).
6. `surface_filter.cpp`: removes the supported near-floor surface (<= 20 m) by longitudinal cell, removal height 0.16 m.
7. `cloud_processing`/`detector_node.cpp`: clustering with radius 0.30 + 0.006 * d (cap 0.6 m); infrastructure filter for long low clusters overlapping rails.
8. `obstacle_evidence.cpp`: separate branch on unclipped current-frame points; geometry classes (tall/small/low/cable) x position classes (center/edge/on_rail/outside); edge candidates checked by `side_surface.cpp:check_side_surface_continuation`; far (>= 35 m) needs two-wall support.
9. `rail_crossbar.cpp:find_rail_crossbar`: 0.5 m longitudinal cells, three zones (left rail, centre, right rail), raised points relative to local neighbour percentiles (excluding own cells), shape and height-consistency checks.
10. Confirmation: 4 frames to confirm, 2 eligible frames for the strict alarm (height >= 0.5 m, lateral span >= 0.18 m, |centre| <= 0.8 m, >= 20 points), clear after 2 frames.

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags
- Rule-stack size and in-sample tuning: `doubleT_obstacle` has its own profile with hand-set `corridor.center_lateral_m=2.0` and `bottom_z_m=-2.3` (docs section 3); the README says this is not a universal calibration.
- Numbers are not reproducible from the repo: section 8 says the result files are not included; the synthetic set comes from an external generator (`T1ray/synthetic_data_generation`).
- Declared-but-unused config: `obstacle_evidence.working.*` is accepted but ignored by `obstacle_evidence()` (docs 6.4); legacy `voxel_leaf_size_m` is only mapped (`detector_node.cpp:120-121`); node defaults (3 frames, 1 eligible) differ from the documented 4 and 2 (`default.yaml:171-173`).
- Far voxels at 0.20 m beyond 50 m can merge the 1-3 return points of small far objects before any rule sees them; the raw evidence branch is the mitigation.
- Beyond the confirmed rail route (or an ambiguous switch) the only alarm allowed is a large isolated obstacle confirmed by two walls (docs section 5), so recall past a switch is limited by design.

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing
- Sequential vs streaming benchmark modes with `/input_sequence` and `/output_sequence` counters and a `/deadline_missed` flag (`tools/benchmark_frame_metrics.py`, `scripts/collect_metrics.py`). This makes frame loss in ROS replay visible; ReSense has no equivalent counter in `resense/metrics.py` or `cli.py` (grep `sequence|drop|deadline`: none relevant).
- Negative-control design in synthetic tests: empty scene and rails-only scene must score 0/24 (`experiments/synthetic_56_*.yaml`, section 8). Worth copying for ReSense's injection runs.
- `benchmark_obstacle_evidence.py` runs the same bag with evidence on and off, a cheap A/B for any single rule.
