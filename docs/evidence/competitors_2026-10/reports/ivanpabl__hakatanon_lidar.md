# ivanpabl/hakatanon_lidar

Team "Golubi" (Russian-language docs). Python core `core/tunnel_od` (~2.5k LOC), ROS 2 node, C++ cloud preprocessor, big `evaluation/` suite. Code license: view/evaluate only (all rights reserved), so take ideas, not code.

## Verdict
Classical geometry, no learning, very close in architecture to ReSense (rail-pair trace -> wall-extended axis -> corridor -> clusters -> evidence tracker -> STOP/CAUTION/clear/unknown). Mature, honest docs, numbers reproduce from the committed CSVs (checked: 191/201 STOP on doubleT_obstacle, 9 STOP frames / 2287 empty frames, new_data 289 STOP frames = 2.6 %, 57 episodes). Trust: medium-low as evidence of generalisation. All rules were tuned on the same 6 recordings plus the organizer bag; `new_data` (the 20-min ride) was "held out" only nominally, since every variant was compared on 8 segments of it. Their own README says "no honest number on foreign data".

Reference points vs ReSense (same recordings, different episode definitions):
- 5 empty recordings (2287 frames): they have 8 STOP episodes / 9 frames, we have 12 episodes. On squareT_platform_squareT_switch they have 3 STOP episodes against our 11. But they tuned on exactly these files.
- 20-min ride: they have 57 episodes over 11.2 km by their own odometry (about 5 per km). We have about 2.3 per km. So on the larger, less-tuned set they are worse than us.
- Their CAUTION share is 28.5 % on empties and 32 % on new_data, which is lower than our 35-69 %.
- Their range claim (person confirmed at 160 m from a 170 m start, 72 % of 120 injected trials, median 118 m) is NOT comparable to our 60/80/100/130/160 m table. The object is injected on the detector's own axis, so axis error is excluded (their own admission, algorithm.md "Ограничения"). Their own axis range (`path_range_m` in `evaluation/reference/alarms_final*.csv`) has median 104-136 m per recording and p10 about 90 m, so the same `beyond_axis` limit applies to them.

## Pipeline
1. `pointcloud.py:parse_pointcloud2`: drop (0,0,0) slots, dedupe Hesai dual return (column pairs, <1 cm); `beam_directions` rebuilds ray directions, including no-return rays. Used only by the injection simulator.
2. `geometry/rails.py`: bed-profile bumps (1 m slices, 5 cm bins, 5 cm prominence). A seed rail pair within 10 m (gauge 1.45-1.75 m, mid <0.4 m from the lidar) is traced forward, each rail with a 12 cm gate and an 8 m gap limit. Axis = polynomial midline.
3. `geometry/path.py:extend_path_by_walls`: axis beyond the rails (38-40 m) is kept at the measured wall distance in 4 m steps (parabola predictor, support point = where the beam actually hit the wall), then `far_axis`: one parabola fitted to far wall points, curvature capped at R>=250 m.
4. `geometry/bed.py`: per-frame bed height vs range (10th percentile per 4 m bin, a bin is accepted only if it continues the Theil-Sen line with grade <=6 %); rail head = bed + measured offset.
5. `detection/detector.py:check_frame`: zone = 0.15-3.0 m over rail head, +-1.0 m (far zone beyond rails end+20 m: +-0.7 m, top 1.5 m); near-field union with the lidar axis (<40 m, straight only). `clustering.py`: gap clustering (1 m fwd, 0.6 m lat), `min_points_at = max(3, 12000/d^2)`, `mark_edge_lines` (chains of edge clusters >8 m = structure).
6. `tracking.py:EvidenceTracker`: gated by predicted train displacement (`geometry/ego_motion.py`: unwrapped-tunnel range image matched against frame t-5); score += n/expected (cap 1.5), decay 0.8, threshold 2.2 and >=3 hits; or "persistent static track" (>=6 hits, Theil-Sen slope of distance vs travel in [-1.25,-0.75]); alarm hold 3 frames; ego-carried veto (slope > -0.35).
7. `plausibility.py` + `decision.py`: per-object context features. Rules `surface_behind` (behind_n) and `front_lift` demote a STOP to CAUTION. `beyond_path`, `ego_carried`, `pending` follow. Status = stop > unknown (sight <30 m or no axis) > caution > clear. `sight_m` includes a curve chord limit sqrt(8 R 1.5).

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags
- Axis and range claims are optimistic: injected objects sit on the detector's own axis; far zone is only +-0.7 m wide, so edge-of-gauge objects at range are only CAUTION by design ("Объекты у края габарита ... только ВНИМАНИЕ").
- Their own ego odometry reads 9.7 km (earlier run) / 11.2 km (final CSV) on the 13.0 km ride; the discrepancy is unexplained and their self-label tool flags itself unreliable because of it. Ours is closer to 13 km, so keep our ego estimate.
- 20-min ride: about 5 STOP episodes per km, about twice ours. Their low numbers on the 5 empties are tuned in-sample.
- Zone width 2.0 m (not 2.1 m) and far top 1.5 m were chosen to suppress false STOP: this trades true recall at gauge edge and for hanging things (they document it: object 5 vs 4 conflict, "cable on ceiling not detected far"). Our 2.1 m + 0.35 m warning band is the more faithful choice.
- Ego-carried veto explicitly fails when the train is nearly stopped (1-3 m/s lost an object at 87-93 m for 3.5 s) and for a person walking away.
- No intensity, no per-ring checks in the decision (computed, unused), no low-object stage: objects below 0.15 m over the rail head are invisible by construction (we have `lowobj.py`).

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing
- `evaluation/eval_injection.py`: runs the same detector on the same window WITHOUT injection and marks a trial "ambiguous" if the detector already sees something at that place; excluded from the found-rate. Cheap fix for our injected-person tables (check whether `resense eval` does this).
- `evaluation/alarms.py` + `selflabel.py`: STOP episode defined per track_id with gap <=5 frames; episode labelled `false_proven` when the odometer later passes the world position (travel + distance + 3 m + 2 %) without a stop. Useful ONLY for unlabeled recordings; on our known-empty 13 km ride every episode is false anyway. Needs good odometry, so low priority.
- `evaluation/eval_fake_obj.py` + `reference/cloud_with_fake_obj/objects.yaml`: per-object first-STOP distance table with the expected level from the organizers' list (STOP for 1,2,3,4,6,8,9,10; not STOP for 5,7). Our `labels/cloud_with_fake_obj.json` already uses the same tail-of-buffer/intensity==1 trick; compare ours against theirs. Useful fact from their yaml: the generator keeps object z constant in the sensor frame, so on slopes far objects float 1.3-1.5 m above (object 1 at 80-120 m) or sink; this makes far synthetic objects non-comparable to real ones.
- `evaluation/reference/*.csv` (per-frame status/distance/path_range for all 7 recordings) can serve as a cross-check baseline for false-STOP frames on identical data.
