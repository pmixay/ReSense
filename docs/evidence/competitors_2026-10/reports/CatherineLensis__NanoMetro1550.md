# CatherineLensis/NanoMetro1550

## Verdict
Hybrid: classical slice geometry and gauge for candidates, plus a hand-written ordered rule list and a one-class Mahalanobis scorer on 9 cluster descriptors; Python + Docker; prototype. The '100 % TPR' is synthetic-only, the honest per-frame FPR on the 20-min ride is 5.52 % (383/6936, in-sample), and `test_and_verify.py` uses self-generated labels.

## Pipeline
1. `tier1_curvilinear_detector.py:process_frame` L186-196: finite and range > 0.3 m filter, self-return sphere around roof bracket (centre (1.55, -1.40, 0.50), r 0.6 m).
2. Bore monitor L200-208: frame max range vs median of last 20 frames; drop > 35 % with baseline > 25 m sets `bore_clear = False`.
3. Per-slice (1 m in Y) tunnel centre from 1.5 / 98.5 percentile mid when single bore (width 4.5-6.5 m, >= 200 pts, L322); otherwise continuity with drift <= 0.15 m per slice; EMA alpha 0.15; stale count 10; forced full refresh every 15 frames (L274).
4. Rail height prior -1.075 m (+/- 0.35) with per-slice 85th percentile of the floor band.
5. Candidate test (L244-270): dz >= 0.22 m (`OBSTACLE_MIN_HEIGHT`), dz <= 3.3 m (2.3 m where slice not bore-confirmed), |dx| <= 1.10 m below 0.43 m and <= 1.45 m above, y in [-60, -2.5] m, minus guard-rail zone and platform-edge zone.
6. Voxel-graph connected components (cKDTree, <= 2 ms) on candidates (>= 15 points).
7. `tier2_classifier.py:classify_cluster`: ordered first-match rules return NOT obstacle for guard rail (L58), cable (L68), SCB cabinet, platform edge, ballast (L104), pillar (L121).
8. Person rule (extents 0.25-1.2 x 0.25-1.2 x 0.75-2.2 m) -> hazard; else one-class Mahalanobis on 9 features, threshold 4.655 -> hazard.
9. `hybrid_pipeline.py` L95-102: per frame, no temporal filter. CRITICAL if distance < 35 m else WARNING. Bore-drop with no cluster reports the frame's max range as the hazard distance.

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags
- The task's minimum object cannot become a candidate: `tier1_curvilinear_detector.py:244` requires dz >= 0.22 m, so a 0.10 m object has no points in that band. The test objects are 0.4 m debris and an 0.8 x 0.6 x 0.9 m box (`test_and_verify.py` L30-60, L147).
- Rules delete clusters: Tier 2 returns 'not obstacle' for anything >= 1.30 m wide (`tier2_classifier.py:121`), for long thin clusters (L68), and for low planar clusters (ballast, L104). The guard-rail zone (L248-254) and platform-edge zone (L262-268) also cut into the in-gauge region, and the 2.3 m station ceiling removes tall objects.
- Single-frame alarm with a bogus distance: no temporal confirmation in the hybrid pipeline or ROS node; the bore-drop case reports the frame's max range as the hazard distance (`hybrid_pipeline.py:102`).
- Thresholds and FPR come from the same ride (chunks 0-181 and 0-135), and the log admits per-chunk results compensate each other.
- Unverifiable inputs: the labels (`new_data/labeling.zip`, 75 KatLab projects) are referenced but not in the repo, and the model is loaded with `allow_pickle=True` (`tier2_classifier.py:21`).

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing
- Per-chunk FPR tables instead of one average, and a dated experiments log that lists failed approaches (`EXPERIMENTS_LOG.md`). Their own "aggregate can hide compensating effects" lesson is the useful part.
- Time-ordered train/val/test split with counts (6817/2153/2095) for any one-class or learned scorer.
- Keep synthetic insertions for validation only, never for fitting. Their PointNet experiment learned generator artifacts. Add a realism check (the organizers' validation bug) before trusting synthetic TPR.
- 12-scenario table format (`test_and_verify.py`) is convenient, but the expected labels are self-generated, so treat it as a smoke test only.
