# Matymij/MetroLiDAR_Sentinel_Humble

## Verdict
Classical geometry, prototype maturity: fixed-frame gauge box + range-layered voxel/DBSCAN + hand-written size/shape rules + hit-count tracking. No calibration, no ground/rail/axis fit, no ego-motion in the detector. Claimed metrics (86 "DANGEROUS objects", max range 188.2 m, 86-164 fps) are NOT trustworthy: no ground truth matching, no FPR, no held-out/ablation; "objects" are mostly tunnel structure. Nothing here that ReSense lacks. Of ~27k LOC, ~24k are archived near-duplicate pipeline versions; the real core is ~1050 lines (`scripts/offline_pipeline_v23_FAST.py`) plus `scripts/plausibility.py`, `confidence.py`, `path_analysis.py`.

## Pipeline (<=10 lines)
1. `offline_pipeline_v23_FAST.py:rotate_roi_numba`: hard-coded axis swap (LiDAR forward = -Y), ROI x 0.3-230, |y|<=3, z -0.3..4.9. Assumes rail at z=0 in lidar frame (`RAIL_Z=0`), gauge fixed +-1.35 m, z 0.2-3.4 m, centred on sensor. No mount calibration, no per-frame floor/rail fit.
2. Split into 3 range layers (near <50, mid 50-120, far >120 m): voxel 0.15/0.30/0.40 and DBSCAN eps 0.5/1.2/4.0 (`cluster_layer`, `grid_dbscan_numba`). Separate overhang pass for z>3.5 m.
3. `classify()` into zones INSIDE/NEAR/RAIL/ABOVE/OUTSIDE/BELOW from cluster bbox centre; `is_valid_object()` = range-banded min points (5->2 with range), cube/long-flat shape test <60 m, size caps; `bbox_intersects_gauge()` with 5/10/15 cm range margin (adds "rail_contact" shortcut).
4. `plausibility.py:check_plausibility`: reject thin (<3 cm / <10 cm >60 m), extreme aspect >35, ceiling, lateral wall slice, horizontal slab, hanging cable (sy<0.1 and z>2.2), ghost.
5. `Tracker`: greedy 3D-nearest association (6 m gate!), confirm at hits>=15, no velocity/Kalman, no range-shrink check.
6. Offline post-hoc stage (`main`): per-track filtering at END of the run (`min_hits_for` 50/30/15/8 by distance, confidence cut, merge tracks within 3 m, zone caps, top-12 "DANGEROUS" chosen with distance-stratified bands). Output is a ranked object list per bag, not a per-frame GO/STOP stream, so it is non-causal and not a real-time decision.
7. `path_analysis.py`: quadratic fit to mean floor-point y per 7.5 m band -> STRAIGHT/GENTLE/SHARP; only effect is scaling `min_hits_for`. ROS node (`detector_node.py`) is an older simplified copy of the same logic. ML (LightGBM) only in `_archive/ml_experiment/`.

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags (<=5 lines)
- Fixed gauge in sensor frame with `RAIL_Z=0`, no mount calibration, no axis/curve model: any pitch/roll, platform, switch, double track shifts the gauge; curvature is only used to scale a post-hoc hit threshold.
- Evaluation is not evidence: no GT. On the one real positive (`results/v21_FINAL/hackathon/doubleT_obstacle.log`) the top list is 34.5 m (1.1x2.1x2.4 m, likely structure), 44.6, 82.9, 27.7, 40.3 m; nothing identifiable at the true ~55-57 m person/object. `cloud_with_fake_obj` "26 DANGEROUS, max 188.2 m" is not matched to the 10 known synthetic objects; the 188.2 m "max range" is one 5-point 0.14x0.41x0.88 m cluster (hits=26), 86 "unique objects" over mostly empty recordings = unmeasured, very likely high FP. README/FINAL_REPORT disagree with each other (70 vs 26 objects, v6/v11/v21/v23 naming, 12 vs 6-voxel numbers) and say "false alarms on switches are not penalised".
- Not a decision system: confirmation is global per-track hit counts (15-50) applied after the whole bag, non-contiguous hits allowed, 6 m association gate, no time-to-confirm, no GO/CAUTION/STOP, no FAULT handling; claimed 86-164 fps is offline batch with 8-16 threads.
- Hard rejection (rules 4/6 in `plausibility.py`, `is_valid_object` "outside_far", "inside_wide" etc.) deletes candidates, including thin hanging cable, against our no-delete safety policy; hard-coded thresholds tuned on the same 3 datasets.

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing (<=5 lines)
- Nothing worth borrowing. `scripts/scan_range.py` (range histogram of points >t m) and `diagnose.py`/`inspect_detector.py` are trivial; `rosbags`-based pure-Python bag reader (`stream_frames`, handles point_step 16/32/other) is equivalent to what we have. Their reject_stats counter per reason (`reject_stats` in `_process_frame_impl`) is a minor diagnostic pattern we already have in reason codes.
