# zeezz108/LCT2026_KIWI7200

## Verdict
Classical geometry, C++17 ROS 2 core (~6k LOC C++ + tools), no learned parts, the closest sibling to ReSense in philosophy (rails -> axis, walls -> far axis, envelope 2.1x3.0, 3-of-5 tracker) and the most rigorous of the three repos. Claimed: 0.15 % false DANGER frames (21 / 13 558 empty frames, incl. the 20-min ride: 19 / 11 271), person on axis 100 % to 125 m and 91-100 % at 150 m on ray-cast synthetic, real person at 56 m kept with no gaps. Trust: moderate. FPR is in-sample (thresholds were tuned on exactly these frames, e.g. low-cluster rise 0.20 -> 0.26 m "because of the 20-min ride"); synthetic recall uses objects placed on the axis found by the detector on the object-free pass; the one real obstacle has 2 people + 1 small object. No result files are in the repo (data/ is git-ignored), so nothing could be re-counted. Their own doc is unusually honest about failure modes.

## Pipeline
1. Pre: dedupe dual returns, auto forward axis, RANSAC bed plane 3-20 m -> sensor height/pitch/roll every frame (`core/ground_calibration.cpp`), cloud into "track frame".
2. Near axis: per-ring cross-section min/max profile (4 cm cells) -> 2-rail template (gauge 1.52, head +0.21 m) 3-32 m, parabola RANSAC (`core/track_axis.cpp`).
3. Far axis + bed profile: `core/corridor_estimator.cpp:searchLateral/searchVertical` beam search (24 beams) over piecewise-constant curvature on segments 0-10-20-35-60-100-150-200 m. Cost = long along-track structures inside the clearance tube - reward where a wall line is found at its near-field offset + rail residuals (with lookahead for pruning) + smoothness. Near-optimal alternatives give axis spread; `evidence_range` = last row where walls confirm the axis; `blockedRange` = tunnel turned out of sight.
4. `core/obstacle_detector.cpp:detect`: per point DANGER / WARNING / low / very-low zone in (lat, h) relative to axis and bed profile, shrink of the envelope by axis uncertainty (0.001 m/m inside evidence, 0.010 m/m beyond, cap 0.9 m); voxel-hash Euclidean clustering; rejection reasons: sparse (beam-geometry density model), floating (only >80 m), flat ring, low-above-local-floor, structure, far-touch.
5. `core/tracker.cpp`: 3-of-5 (+1 per 25 m beyond 50 m, max 5), weak clusters need 6-of-10, strong (>=40 pts, <40 m) at once, DANGER needs >=2 danger hits.
6. Outputs CLEAR / WARNING / DANGER, free_distance, TTC. 23 ms/frame, 4 OpenMP threads.

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags
- Very-low object (0.3 x 0.3 x 0.1 m): 12-25 m only, WARNING level only, between rails only (86 % at 20 m, 0 % at 25-30 m); ReSense confirms small objects to 30-56 m. Low objects not checked beyond 75 m.
- FPR numbers are in-sample and mix tuning with reporting; "15 episodes" (section 2.1) vs "five episodes" + "six episodes" (section 2) are inconsistent; WARNING is on 3 % of frames. No per-km figure; STOP-equivalent here also needs >=2 danger hits.
- Axis: their own uncertainty estimate has ~0 correlation with real error; p90 axis error 1.16 m at 100 m on own data; a 1.0 m offset object at 100 m is found in 0-86 % of cases depending on segment (new_curve 0 %). yaml comment says envelope never narrower than +-0.45 m but `lateral_shrink_max = 0.9` leaves +-0.15 m (code/doc mismatch in `obstacle_detector.hpp:60` vs detector.yaml).
- Far "floating" rule (cluster bottom >1.2 m above bed) only beyond 80 m; "thin hanging" shape never downgraded near; no multi-signature infrastructure vocabulary like ours.
- Real obstacle evidence is one recording with a standing train; no accumulated-frame benefit (their accumulation was measured and switched off: first DANGER 141 -> 164 m but FPR 0.15 -> 0.4-0.88 %).

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing
- `tools/summarize_run.py` (episodes of alarms on the long ride, gap-merged) - same idea as our episode counting; compare definitions before comparing numbers (their 15 DANGER episodes / 20 min ~ 1.2 per km if 13 km, vs our ~2.3).
- `tools/axis_error.py` + `tools/ego_motion.py` path-as-ground-truth method (idea 6) and `tools/profile_error.py` (bed profile vs peak of height histogram per range bin) as label-free diagnostics of the two quantities that gate far detection.
- `tools/sensor_check.py`: verifies the beam model against the datasheet (64 dense channels 0.1 x 0.122 deg, 64 sparse; working range comes from the dense half) - confirms beam-geometry assumptions used by density thresholds.
- `tools/benchmark.py --baseline <tag>`: one command that prints FPR, timing and synthetic recall differences against a tagged previous config (we have `scripts/regression_gate.py`).
