# templeJJs/test_lidar

Scope note: 75k LOC is inflated. `track_geometry.py` (9.5k) is duplicated byte-identical in `pavel/test/`; `rail_detection.py`, `visualize_bag*.py` exist in 3-4 copies (`pavel/old`, `external/`); `web_viewer.py`, `labeling.py`, `app/`, `sim/`, `check_*.py` are viewer/labelling/sim tooling. Detector cores actually read: `pavel/guard/*` (the shipped "guard_detector", ~6.5k LOC net of plot/eval scripts), `pavel/odometry.py`, `pavel/speed_estimator.py`, and the older baseline `detector/*` + `docs/detector-*.md` + `validation/*`. No bag data in the repo (11 MB total), so nothing could be re-verified numerically; all numbers below are README/docs claims, cross-checked only against code comments.

## Verdict
Classical geometry, no ML ("explain the normal tunnel, alarm on the unexplained residual in the gauge"). Mid-maturity, heavily hand-tuned; the README itself admits the shipped pipeline is not at its limit (multi-frame layer disabled, 36 confirmed false episodes / 1510 frames, only 6 of 8 mandatory synthetic objects, person found at 52-58 m on a nearly standing train so it is not a range test). Their corridor "reach 100-115 m" is wall-confirmed free space, not obstacle detection range. Claims are in-sample and mostly un-logged (guard numbers exist only in README prose; only the older `detector/` has CSV evidence in `validation/out/`). Weaker than ReSense on range and on evaluation breadth; few transferable techniques.

## Pipeline
1. `pavel/guard/track.py`: rail-head pair per 4 m slice, branch continuity (own pair repeats across slices), straight axis, zone 7-30 m.
2. `check_wall.py` + `wall_rules.py` (+ `axis_wall.py`): wall lines per 0.5 m height layer; per-5 m-bin axis mode RAILS/CENTER/LEAD/HOLD (wall edge + per-frame measured wall-to-track offset), up-switch needs several consecutive bins.
3. `walls.py`: corridor = inner edge (10th pct / Harrell-Davis hybrid) of the worst wall layer within train height; `uncovered` mask from a Pandar128 ring model; `accum.py` votes corridor bins across 6 frames.
4. `structures.py`: cells (0.4 m lat x 0.5 m up) occupied in >=3 of 5 consecutive 5 m bins at the same lateral offset = tunnel structure (explained); tall column cells explained; wide continuous low sheet kept as obstacle.
5. `obstacles.py`: overlapping 5 m windows (2.5 m step), cluster of the residual with >=2.5x excess over 40th-pct local background, >=12 pts, >=0.25 m high; explain-rules column / wall-ripple / dust / not-on-bed / fragment.
6. `tracker.py`: world-frame (SE(2) poses from `trajectory.npz`) tracks, confirm at 2 frames, static-in-world check, mass escalation, duplicate-hit dedup. The ROS node (`guard_node.py`) has NO poses/odometry, so in the Docker deliverable the tracker degrades to sensor frame (README plan item 2 admits it).
Baseline `detector/core.py`: per-recording stored axis polyline (`track_models.json`) + free-volume bins + (y,u) occupancy connected-component wall test.

## Things done worse than ReSense / red flags
- Baseline `detector/` is overfit by construction: a hand-built per-recording axis polyline (`detector/track_models.json`, `build_models.py`) and `valid_far_m` shortened step by step "until the frames of the recording have no false alarms", so "0 FP on 2287 empty frames" is in-sample; it is a prebuilt map, unusable on unseen track. Its own injection curve: P(detect) 85.6 % at 25 m, 52.8 % at 50 m, 0 beyond 75 m (docs/detector-results.md 9.2).
- Deployed `guard_node.py` has no odometry: world-frame tracker, static check and accumulation are offline-only (`trajectory.npz` precomputed from the bag). README lists online odometry as a TODO.
- Far axis (LEAD/HOLD) error exceeds the half-gauge yet reach 100-115 m is advertised; `README` "stable at 52-58 m" is on a standing train (11 m travelled in 201 frames), no range-to-first-STOP measurement on a moving train, no FPR per km, no recall on held-out objects. Offline eval credits track hits retroactively (`validation/dataset_eval.py --temporal`, `TemporalResult.retro`).
- Docs contradict code: README/obstacles docstring say the gauge top is 2.50 m and the `column` rule "never fires", but `walls.py` GAUGE_H_HI = 3.00 (stale text). Many `DUST_EXC_*`/`SLAB_*` constants are carved around individual frames of `cloud_with_fake_obj` (f581, f624, f676, f789): in-sample tuning on the organizer synthetic. Hard `dust` and `base>0.5 m` rules delete (not downgrade) candidates, which conflicts with our safety stance.
- Repo hygiene: ~30k LOC are duplicates/viewer code; no tests for `pavel/guard` (tests/ cover `detector/`, sim, labels only).

## Eval/data tooling worth borrowing
- Scene-clustered confidence intervals for injection trials, unique seed per trial, and reporting "visible" (`rays_hit>0`) next to "detected" so sensor limit and detector limit are separated (`validation/synth.py:scene_ci`, `validation/detector_curve.py`, `docs/detector-results.md` 9.2). Our eval has no cluster/Wilson CI (grep): worth adding to the 15-trial range table, whose scenes share frames.
- FP/h framing with episodes vs frames, Poisson upper bound (rule of three) and "hours needed to prove 1e-2 / 1e-4 FP/h" per Tagiew arXiv 2307.02586 (`validation/fp_per_hour.py`, results 2.2-2.3); our `docs/RESEARCH.md` cites the paper but no run reports the bound.
- Pseudo ground truth for the axis from the train's own later path (`pavel/guard/eval_axis_far.py`, `eval_wall_corridor.py`): lateral axis error at d metres vs where the train later actually drove. Not a detector (we rejected maps), but a usable evaluation of our `axis_valid`/`beyond_axis` trust on the 13 km ride, where truth is free.
- `pavel/guard/metrics/episodes.py` (collapse detections to tracks for labelling) and `validation/dataset_eval.py` range-bucket recall: we already have equivalents, nothing new.
- `sim/` (open3d raycast tunnel simulator with Pandar128 model) is weaker than our `resense/synthetic.py` (injection into real frames with the sensor's own ray grid); skip.
