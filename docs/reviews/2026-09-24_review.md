## 4. Findings

Severity is the reviewer's estimate of the effect on the organizers' scoring.

**F1 — high (8.1, 8.2, 8.4). Objects lying on the bed between the rails are never reported, and
the envelope floor that causes it is the team's assumption.**

- The strict envelope is `|dy| ≤ 1.05 m`, 0.12–3.0 m above the rail head (`configs/default.yaml:82-90`).
  In these tunnels the bed lies 0.26–0.34 m below the rail head (EXPERIMENTS.md §2d, round 2,
  reading 5).
- Set F round 3 (`experiments_2026-09-24_remeasure.json` → `setF.small_bed`), on the bed with the
  defaults: a 30 × 30 × 10 cm box 0 of 6 approaches, a 30 cm cube 1 of 6 (at 9 m), a dog-sized
  0.6 × 0.3 × 0.45 m box 1 of 6 (at 50 m). At station stops the dog-sized box is 1 of 6 as well
  (`setF.stops`).
- The organizers said:
  - "anything in the train envelope, at least 30 × 30 × 10 cm, is an obstacle" (transcript [49:56], line 105);
  - "someone may throw a dog" ([48:55], line 103);
  - "we also have a tool that makes synthetic obstacles, with which we will check the solutions" ([07:32], line 23).
- The organizers never gave the envelope's lower edge. `docs/QUESTIONS.md` does not ask for it.
- ALGORITHM.md §6 and README.md:423 present `lowobj.near_enabled` as the opt-in answer, with its
  real-ride cost "unmeasured". The reviewer measured it (F2).

**F2 — high (8.1, 8.5). The opt-in near-bed path would flood the train with false stops.**

With `lowobj.near_enabled: true` (reviewer's full run over the 13 759 frames):

| | defaults | near-bed path on |
|---|---|---|
| five obstacle-free recordings: alarm frames / events / STOP episodes | 107 / 20 / 27 | 1 035 / 145 / 42 |
| ride: alarm frames / events / STOP episodes | 204 / 47 / 39 | 3 730 (33 % of the ride) / 667 / 247 |
| person 58 / 61, object 124 / 126 | unchanged | unchanged |

- 626 of the 667 ride events are low-object tracks: median first distance 19 m (10–90 %: 8–28 m),
  median |lateral| 0.02 m. These are the central bed fixtures of EXPERIMENTS.md §1d, such as
  train-control inductors and drain covers.
- On set F (reviewer's run, bed placement from 150 m):
  - the dog-sized box is found in 5 of 6 approaches from ~20 m (19.5–50.2 m);
  - the 30 cm cube in 2 of 6 (9–15 m);
  - the 30 × 30 × 10 cm box in 0 of 6;
  - 69–75 false detections appear per set, against 0–6 with the defaults.
- The path is correctly off by default (`configs/default.yaml:211`), but it is not a fix to
  switch on. Bed objects need a different discriminator (SCORECARD improvement 10).

**F3 — high (8.1). The false-stop rate on obstacle-free data is high for a train.**

- Ride: 39 STOP episodes in ~20 minutes, one every ~31 s. Its 47 events by cause (EXPERIMENTS.md §0, `labels/new_data_objects.json`):
  corridor-edge structures 18, bed-level fixtures 11, far small clusters 7.
- Five recordings: 25 of the 27 STOP episodes and 15 of the 20 events come from
  `squareT_platform_squareT_switch` (877 frames, ~88 s); 10 of those 15 events are first
  confirmed beyond 100 m (reviewer's recount).
- Starting 0–40 frames late gives 14–20 events (team record, `experiments_v0.6.3_start_offsets.json`).

**F4 — medium (8.2). All long-range evidence is synthetic, with a placement derived from the
detector's own far model.**

- `scripts/far_range_eval.py` defaults to `--placement-mode legacy` (lines 12–16 of its
  docstring). EVALUATION.md §3 says it "can flatter recall on curves and at the envelope edge".
- The independent mode exists but has never been run (EXPERIMENTS.md "Sprint 3 protocol").
- The reviewer's re-run of the person approach on straight track reproduces the order of
  magnitude: median first confirmation 147 m (110, 146, 147, 148, 148, 158 m), held from 151 m.
- The strict "every 10 m band ≥ 90 %" measure is 115 m for a person, 105 m for a crate and 70 m
  for a trolley.
- Small objects on a rail head are first found at 42 m (the 10 cm box) and 49 m (the cube).
- The only real obstacle stands at 55–57 m.

**F5 — medium (8.3, 8.6). The start of a played bag is lost for longer than the docs say.**

- The stored node logs show these holes in the header stamps after the first frame:

  | log | holes | total |
  |---|---|---|
  | `ct_real_node.log`, `roundT_doubleT` | 2.7 s + 1.8 s | 4.5 s |
  | `ct_real_node.log`, `doubleT_obstacle` (UDP profile) | 2.2 s + 1.3 s + 1.4 s | 4.9 s |
  | `obstacle_node.log` (before the UDP profile) | 4.0 s + 2.0 s | 6.0 s |
  | `rviz_chain_node.log` (bag at 0.5×) | 1.1 s | 1.1 s |

- The docs say 2–4 s (README.md:35, README.md:227, SUBMISSION.md:89, SUBMISSION.md:104).
  EXPERIMENTS.md:1060 says "0.9 s in our run" for the 120° recording, while the team's own
  `ct_real` run on that recording lost 4.5 s.
- Every hole above `hole_reset_gap` (1 s) resets the scene (`detector_node.py:401-406`). For a
  20 s control recording this is 22–30 % of it.
- The fix is in progress elsewhere; the docs should then quote the measured value.

**F6 — medium (8.3). 360° input is not real-time on 4 vCPU, and half of it is behind the train.**

- `ct_real_node.log` for `doubleT_obstacle`: 4.5–10.0 fps per 2 s window, p95 99–151 ms.
  `checks.txt` (dry run): 103 of 201 frames processed, 86 dropped, `FAIL: p95 latency 130 ms > 100 ms`.
- Offline, the track stage costs 39.9 ms on the 347 k-point 360° frames against 27.4 ms on the
  189 k-point 120° frames (`evidence/timing_2026-09-23/bench_v063.txt`).
- No stage drops the points behind the train: `resense/frame.py:63` and
  `resense/pointcloud.py:76` crop by range only. The mount calibration needs the whole cloud to
  find the orientation, but after that the rear half is dead weight.
- The i7-9700E stand has never been measured.

**F7 — medium (8.6). CI's "organizers' way" does not test a stock host console.**

- `scripts/console_test.sh:63` runs the player from the project image. That image sets
  `FASTRTPS_DEFAULT_PROFILES_FILE` to the UDP-only profile (`docker/Dockerfile:71`), so both sides
  of the CI test run the same non-default transport.
- The organizers will "most likely play the bag from the console" (answers.md, written answer 2),
  that is, with stock Fast DDS (shared memory + UDP) or another RMW.
- It should work: the node only announces UDP locators. But no CI step and no stored evidence
  file shows it; the only statement was in a deleted review of 23.09.

**F8 — low (8.1 as the jury sees it). `CAUTION` is the most common state of an empty tunnel.**

- `CAUTION` covers 26.8–67.9 % of the frames of the obstacle-free recordings and 41.1 % of the ride
  (reproduced).
- While the input is silent, `/resense/obstacle_detected` is `false` and `/resense/decision` is
  `FAULT` (`detector_node.py:471-475`, `:535-543`).
- Both are documented in README.md "What to look at", so this is a presentation risk, not a defect.

**F9 — low (8.4). Scene dependence of the false alarms and of the calibration.**

- 15 of the 20 events on the five recordings come from one station recording (F3). The pieces
  of the ride with station stops had the most events before v0.6.2 (20 and 18 of 82, EXPERIMENTS.md §0).
- The final tilt calibration needs 20 observations every 10 frames (ALGORITHM.md §2b); only a tilt
  above 2.5° is corrected provisionally after 5 frames.
- The organizers say the control data use the mount of the empty-tunnel rides, which the
  calibration finds level, so the risk is small.

**F10 — low (8.5, 8.7). The documentation is hard to read in jury time.**

- `wc -l README.md docs/*.md` at `d58567e` → 4 612 lines. EXPERIMENTS.md has 1 266 lines, and three sprint
  preambles that report "no new measurement" (lines 19–70) come before its §0.
- The current state is spread over the "Re-measurement" table, §0 and §2d round 3.
- Stale statements remain (section 5 below).

**F11 — low (8.8). The pitch material carries outdated numbers and silent videos.**

- Section 6 lists the deck and PRESENTATION.md lines.
- None of the four MP4 files in `docs/video/` has a sound track: no `soun` handler in any of them.

**F12 — information (§7.2).**

- `git tag -l` is empty. The intermediate tag `v0.1-intermediate` that SUBMISSION.md:16-18
  describes was never created, and the final tag is deferred (SUBMISSION.md, "Upload").
- The base image `ros:humble-ros-base-jammy` is not pinned by digest (`docker/Dockerfile:12`);
  the pip packages are pinned exactly (`:46`).

What holds up well, verified (not findings):

- The node's input handling (switching topics and recordings, the watchdog, faults) has 18 tests
  against ROS stand-ins (`tests/test_node.py`).
- The decision logic is simple and matches the README (`detector_node.py:535-543`).
- The parameter file is the single source (Dockerfile copies it over the package copy, CI checks it).
- The mount calibration is checked on re-mounted real frames (EXPERIMENTS.md §6, raw in
  `evidence/mount_check_2026-09-23/`).
- The false-alarm accounting separates events, alarm frames and STOP episodes.
- Every number in EXPERIMENTS.md is marked real or synthetic.
